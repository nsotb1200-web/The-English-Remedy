ABOUT THE ENGLISH REMEDY

Language learning with
healthcare in mind.

The English Remedy is a learning concept for healthcare students at King Abdulaziz University. The goal is to make English practice approachable, structured and relevant to future healthcare professionals.

A learning companion — not a substitute for clinical training or approved course materials.
