# Approved content scope — The English Remedy

This file records the source boundaries supplied for the initial curriculum build.

## Sources and roles

1. **Healthcare & Nursing** (Healthcare - Nursing ٢.pdf) — source for lesson topics and context. Use only Units 1–4.
2. **Medical Terminology Practice Reference** (Medical_Terminology_Practice_Guide(1).pdf) — vocabulary source. Use only Chapter/Part 1 as designated by the project owner; keep English terms paired with the Arabic meanings supplied in the guide.
3. **Healthcare & Nursing Vocabulary** (قاموس مفردات الرعاية الصحية والتمريض PDF) — vocabulary source. Use vocabulary through Unit 4 only. Exclude Unit 5 vocabulary even though it appears in the supplied glossary.

## Learning sections

- **Listening:** create scripts and multiple-choice questions based on the permitted topic material. Clearly label any temporary browser-generated speech as synthetic.
- **Reading:** create short passages and multiple-choice comprehension questions grounded in permitted topics.
- **Vocabulary:** use the two vocabulary references above; retain the supplied spellings and meanings. Do not silently correct or expand the source list.
- **Levels:** A1, A2, B1, and B2 describe activity difficulty, not permission to add topics or terminology outside the approved scope.

## Content integrity rules

- Do not use Healthcare & Nursing Units 5–10.
- Do not use Healthcare & Nursing Vocabulary Unit 5.
- Do not add Medical Terminology chapters/parts beyond the designated first chapter/part.
- Do not claim generated practice passages are quotations from the books. Label them as adapted practice based on approved topics.
- Record the source unit/section for each lesson and each vocabulary entry in the content data where practical.
- If a needed detail is not supported by these files, leave it out or flag it for review rather than filling the gap from general knowledge.

## Current status

The PDFs have been provided in the conversation. They must be parsed and mapped before the full lesson bank and answer keys can be considered verified. This scope document is a boundary, not a claim that all lessons have already been extracted or validated.
